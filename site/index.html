The usual times are one key away

In an hour, this evening, tomorrow morning, next week. Press Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+5, use the arrow keys, or click. Times follow your clock settings, and you decide what “evening” and “morning” mean.

Everything you snoozed, in one place

Click Bring Later in the notification area to see what’s waiting and when it’s due. Bring a window back early, or give it a different time.

Nothing gets lost

A hidden window with no way back would be worse than clutter, so every path ends with your windows on screen.

Quit, sign out, or crash
Every snoozed window comes back first. The snooze is saved before a window is hidden, never after.
The app closes the window
You still get the reminder at the time you picked, so the thought isn’t lost with it.
You need it sooner
Bring it back from the list at any time. If the app shows the window again on its own, the snooze simply ends.
Your computer was asleep
Anything that came due while it slept comes back as soon as it wakes.

Settings that stay out of the way

Change the shortcut, what “this evening” means, and whether returning windows come to the front or wait quietly behind what you’re doing. Light and dark follow Windows.

Download

No installer and no account. Bring Later isn’t code-signed yet, so your system asks once before the first launch.

Windows

Windows 11 · Intel, AMD or Arm

Download for Windows Arm version
  1. Run the downloaded .exe. Bring Later lives in the notification area; nothing is installed.
  2. If SmartScreen appears, select More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Press Win Shift Z on any window.

macOS

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Download for Mac
  1. Unzip and move Bring Later to Applications.
  2. Open it once, then choose Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
  3. Allow Accessibility access, then press ⌃ ⌥ Z on any window.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, and no network requests unless you choose Check for updates. Window titles stay on your computer. The code is on GitHub under the MIT License, so you don’t have to take our word for it.
