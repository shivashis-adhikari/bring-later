Snooze a window until you need it. Press a shortcut, pick a time, and the window disappears. It comes back where it was when the time comes.

**Shortcut:** Win+Shift+Z on Windows, ⌃⌥Z on Mac.

### Download

- **Windows:** `BringLater-0.1.0-win-x64.exe`
- **Windows on Arm:** `BringLater-0.1.0-win-arm64.exe`
- **Mac:** `BringLater-0.1.0-mac.zip`

### First launch

The app isn't signed yet, so you'll see a warning once.

- **Windows:** click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway**, then allow Accessibility access.

### Good to know

- Quitting or signing out brings every snoozed window back.
- Full-screen windows on Mac can't be snoozed yet.
