Bring Later now speaks Simplified Chinese, Spanish and Hindi. It follows your system language and uses English for everything else.

### Download

- **Windows:** `BringLater-0.2.0-win-x64.exe`
- **Windows on Arm:** `BringLater-0.2.0-win-arm64.exe`
- **Mac:** `BringLater-0.2.0-mac.zip`

### What's new

- Both apps in Chinese, Spanish and Hindi.
- Fixed a crash on Mac right after allowing Accessibility access.

### First launch

The app isn't signed yet, so you'll see a warning once.

- **Windows:** click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway**, then allow Accessibility access. After updating, allow it again.
